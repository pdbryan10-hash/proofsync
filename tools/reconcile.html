ProofSync · reconciliation workbench

Match two CAFM exports. Map the fields. Surface every disagreement.

This is what ProofSync does to every job: take the same job from two systems, match it on its reference, line up the fields, and flag anything that disagrees. Upload two exports (say six months from the contractor's Joblogic and the client's Concerto) — or press play and watch it on a worked example.

1 Load your two exports

Two CSV files. First row must be the column headers. Big files are fine — they're parsed locally.

2 Choose the reference to match on

The unique job reference that appears in both systems — the key ProofSync would match on. We've guessed; change it if we're wrong.

3 Map the fields to compare

Each row pairs a System-A column with the System-B column that should hold the same value, and how to compare them. We've auto-suggested from the header names.
