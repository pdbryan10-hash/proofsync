The trade rule — for the conversation with Jeff

Capital pays for the work; recurring pays for the company. The build fee should cover your delivery and keep you liquid. The monthly is the asset — it's what someone buys when they buy ProofWorks, and it's the price OCS and the MML 300 behind them will anchor to.

So when a customer pushes on price — and they will — give away the upfront, never the monthly. "We'll credit the POC and waive half the connector fee" costs you once. "£299 a month" costs you every month, for years, on every deal after it.

How the maths works. Each customer pays the build fee once, then the monthly for as long as they stay — churn leaks the active base away at the rate you set, so recurring, month-12 MRR and valuation are all net of it. Cash is real money: fees in, minus AWS per live client, ops, cash build costs, and your drawings — so the low-point is your household exposure, not just the company's. Your build time is deliberately not a cash cost (you don't pay yourself twice); it lives in hours. Net margin = 12-month revenue minus all of that cash. Company value = churn-adjusted run-rate ARR (month-12 MRR × 12) × multiple, plus one-off revenue × the one-off multiple. Hours required each month = new builds × build hours + live clients × support hours; where it crosses the hours you have is the month you must hire. Excludes VAT; a planning model, not a forecast.

How these figures are worked out. The saving is your own numbers: jobs re-keyed each month × minutes each × your fully-loaded admin rate, plus a rework allowance for entries that are mis-keyed and re-done (10% by default). The saving is net of the exceptions ProofSync can't auto-sync (5% by default), which still need a person. Connector build is £6,000 for the first client system and £3,000 for each additional one. The monthly is a per-pair ratchet — one pair is one client CAFM you sync into: £299 for the first, £200 for each of the next four, then £180 each beyond that. The per-job figure is the current annual admin cost divided by annual jobs, so the yearly total is simply that per-job cost across your own volume — check the one, and the other follows. Figures exclude missed-job and late-payment costs, which ProofSync also reduces, and exclude VAT. A planning estimate to size the opportunity, not a quote.
